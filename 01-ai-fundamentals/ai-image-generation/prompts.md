# Project 02: AI Image Generation — Prompt Documentation

## Project Objective

Use Google Gemini to design, generate, and refine a
cybersecurity awareness poster that teaches employees
how to recognize phishing emails.

## Prompt 1: Creative Brainstorming

I need ideas for a cybersecurity awareness poster
that teaches small business employees how to identify
phishing emails.

The style should be modern and professional, with
a clear educational focus.

Provide three creative concepts, including:

1. Visual layout and composition.
2. Recommended color palette.
3. How each concept communicates cybersecurity risks.

### AI Response

Gemini recommended three concepts:

1. Anatomy of a Phish
2. The Bait and the Catch
3. Pause Before You Click

### My Decision

I selected "Anatomy of a Phish" because it uses a
realistic email example to demonstrate common
phishing warning signs.

---

## Prompt 2: Content Development

Generate three text options for a cybersecurity
awareness poster titled "Anatomy of a Phish."

The target audience is small business employees.

Use clear, professional language that helps
employees recognize suspicious emails.

Include common phishing warning signs and
practical prevention advice.

### AI Response

Gemini provided three approaches:

1. Short and Punchy
2. Balanced and Action-Oriented
3. Descriptive and Educational

### My Decision

I selected the shorter approach because employees
should be able to understand the poster quickly.

---

## Prompt 3: Image Generation

Using the "Anatomy of a Phish" concept,
generate a professional cybersecurity awareness poster.

Include a realistic example of a phishing email.

Highlight four warning signs:

1. Mismatched sender address
2. Fake urgency
3. Suspicious links and attachments
4. Requests for sensitive information

Use navy blue, white, and red.

Include the headline:

"ANATOMY OF A PHISH"

And the subtitle:

"Spot the scam before you click."

Finish with the message:

"When in doubt, reach out."

---

## Prompt 4: First Image Refinement

Review the generated poster for spelling errors,
unreadable text, and overlapping elements.

Improve the readability of the example email.

Maintain the original professional design,
color palette, and four phishing warning signs.

### Evaluation

The initial image contained distorted text and
overlapping annotations.

The revised image improved some elements but
still contained unreadable text.

---

## Prompt 5: Final Image Refinement

Simplify the example phishing email.

Use shorter sentences and clearly readable text.

Remove unnecessary information that creates
visual clutter.

Keep the four warning labels and their arrows.

Ensure that the email example demonstrates
the cybersecurity risks accurately.

### Evaluation

The final version demonstrated improved readability,
a simpler email example, and clearer warning labels.

---

## Lessons Learned

1. Detailed prompts help establish visual direction.

2. AI-generated images require careful review,
   especially when they contain text.

3. Iterative prompting can improve visual quality.

4. Simplifying instructions can reduce errors.

5. Human evaluation remains essential when using
   generative AI for cybersecurity education.
