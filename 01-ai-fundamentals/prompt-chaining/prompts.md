# Prompt Chaining with Google Gemini

## Project Objective

Use prompt chaining to explore AI-enabled business opportunities,
evaluate their feasibility, and develop an implementation plan.

## Prompt 1: Brainstorming

I operate a small business consulting company specializing in
operations management, recruitment, and administrative support.

I want to expand into AI-enabled business services.

Act as my business advisor and recommend three complementary
services we could offer.

Consider that we have limited startup capital, want to operate
remotely, and are developing our technical expertise in
artificial intelligence.

## Prompt 2: Evaluating Options

Of the three services you recommended, which would be the easiest
and most affordable to launch within six months?

Compare startup costs, technical skills required, potential
profitability, and market demand.

Rank them from easiest to most difficult to implement.

Consider that I am currently completing the Google AI Professional
Certificate and training in AI-enabled IT support.

## Prompt 3: Creating an Action Plan

I want to move forward with developing a Custom AI Knowledge Base
and Administrative Copilot service for small businesses.

Create a realistic seven-day action plan for someone currently
learning AI and IT support.

Focus on free tools, practical learning, and building a simple
demonstration project using fictional company documents.

Include daily tasks, expected outcomes, and ways to evaluate
whether the AI provides accurate information.

Do not assume I have advanced programming experience.
