# Project 01: Prompt Chaining with Google Gemini

## Project Objective

Use prompt chaining to explore AI-enabled business opportunities, evaluate their feasibility, and develop a practical implementation plan.

**Course:** Google AI Professional Certificate  
**Module:** AI Fundamentals  
**Lab:** Practice Prompt Chaining in Gemini  
**AI Tool:** Google Gemini

---

## Step 1: Brainstorming Business Opportunities

### Objective
Identify three AI-enabled services that could complement an existing operations management and recruitment consulting business.

### Gemini's Recommendations

**1. AI-Powered Recruitment & Candidate Screening**

Using AI to assist with resume analysis, interview scheduling, and candidate communications.

**2. Custom AI Knowledge Base & Administrative Copilots**

Developing AI assistants that help employees retrieve information from company policies, procedures, and internal documents.

**3. AI Operational Audit & Workflow Automation**

Identifying repetitive business processes and implementing AI-assisted automation to improve operational efficiency.

### Outcome
Gemini identified three potential services that aligned with the business's existing operations experience and remote working model.

---

## Step 2: Evaluating the Recommendations

### Objective
Compare the three services based on startup costs, technical complexity, profitability, and implementation timelines.

### Gemini's Evaluation

| Service | Difficulty | Estimated Launch Time | Estimated Startup Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Recruitment | Low | 2–4 weeks | Nearly $0 |
| AI Knowledge Base | Medium | 1–2 months | Under $100/month |
| AI Workflow Automation | Medium–High | 3–4 months | $100–$300/month |

*Note: These estimates were generated by Gemini and have not been independently verified.*

### Outcome
Gemini ranked AI recruitment as the easiest service to launch, followed by knowledge-base development and workflow automation.

I selected the AI knowledge-base option for further exploration because it combines business operations experience with developing AI and IT support skills.

---

## Step 3: Developing an Implementation Plan

### Objective
Create a seven-day action plan for developing an AI-powered internal knowledge base using fictional business documents.

### Gemini's Proposed Project

**Company:** Apex Heating & Air (Fictional)

**Solution:** AI-powered internal knowledge assistant using Google's NotebookLM.

### Seven-Day Implementation Plan

| Day | Task | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create fictional HR, pricing, and IT support documents | Three source documents |
| 2 | Create a NotebookLM notebook and upload documents | Initial knowledge base |
| 3 | Configure assistant instructions | Defined assistant behavior |
| 4 | Test accuracy and information retrieval | Evaluation results |
| 5 | Generate onboarding materials | Demonstration materials |
| 6 | Prepare a product demonstration | Demo presentation |
| 7 | Develop a proposed service package | Preliminary business offering |

### Outcome
Gemini produced a structured plan for developing and evaluating a prototype AI knowledge assistant.

**Implementation Status:** Proposed only. The seven-day plan has not yet been executed.

## AI Accuracy and Responsible Use

Reviewing Gemini's responses revealed several areas requiring additional verification.

### 1. Business Estimates

Gemini provided specific pricing, profitability, and implementation estimates without supporting evidence.

These figures should be treated as preliminary assumptions rather than established market data.

### 2. Recruitment Risks

Gemini described AI-assisted recruitment as having almost no liability.

However, AI-assisted hiring can introduce discrimination, privacy, and compliance risks.

Human oversight is essential when evaluating employment candidates.

### 3. Knowledge-Base Accuracy

Gemini proposed combining information from different company documents to answer employee questions.

However, combining separate facts does not necessarily establish a valid company policy.

For example, the fictional pricing documents do not clearly establish whether Gold Plan customers must pay an additional emergency service fee.

### 4. Data Security

Using source documents to ground AI responses does not automatically guarantee confidentiality, accuracy, or appropriate access controls.

These considerations require evaluation before implementing an AI solution with actual business information.

## Project Results

The prompt-chaining exercise demonstrated how a complex business planning task can be divided into three connected stages:

1. Generating potential solutions.
2. Evaluating those solutions against practical constraints.
3. Developing an actionable implementation plan.

Each prompt built upon the information generated during the previous stage.

## Skills Practiced

- Prompt engineering
- Prompt chaining
- AI-assisted business analysis

- Project planning
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated information
- Responsible AI awareness

## Key Takeaway

Prompt chaining provides a structured approach to solving complex problems using generative AI.

However, AI-generated recommendations should be evaluated for accuracy, feasibility, and potential risks before being used to make business decisions.
