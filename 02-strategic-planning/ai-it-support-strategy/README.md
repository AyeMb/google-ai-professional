# Project 03: AI-Powered IT Support Strategic Planning

## Overview

This project was completed as part of Course 2 of the
Google AI Professional Certificate.

I used Google Gemini to develop a strategic plan for
an AI-powered IT support service targeting small businesses.

The project demonstrates how generative AI can support
brainstorming, business analysis, industry research,
and workflow automation.

## Project Objectives

- Brainstorm practical AI-powered IT support solutions.
- Compare potential solutions using cost-benefit analysis.
- Research emerging cybersecurity trends.
- Identify a realistic solution for future development.
- Automate recurring industry research.

## Tools Used

- Google Gemini
- AI Prompt Engineering
- Cost-Benefit Analysis
- Gemini Scheduled Actions
- GitHub

## Project Workflow

### 1. Brainstorming

Used Gemini to generate eight AI-powered IT support ideas,
including automated ticket triage, troubleshooting assistants,
phishing detection, and employee onboarding automation.

### 2. Cost-Benefit Analysis

Compared each solution based on:

- Estimated development cost
- Technical difficulty
- Business benefit
- Implementation time
- Portfolio value

### 3. Industry Research

Researched emerging trends in AI-powered email security,
including QR code phishing, phishing-as-a-service,
and AI-assisted threat detection.

Research findings require independent source verification
before being used as established industry statistics.

### 4. Workflow Automation

Configured a recurring Gemini action to research
AI-powered phishing detection and email security.

Schedule: Every Monday at 9:00 AM.

## Selected Solution: AI Phishing Analyzer

Based on the analysis, I selected an AI-assisted
phishing analyzer as a potential future development project.

The proposed solution would:

1. Analyze suspicious email content.
2. Identify potential phishing warning signs.
3. Explain suspicious indicators in plain language.
4. Recommend appropriate verification steps.
5. Support human review rather than make definitive
   safety decisions.

This project focuses on strategic planning.
A functional phishing analyzer has not yet been developed.

## Skills Demonstrated

- AI-assisted strategic planning
- Prompt engineering
- Business decision-making
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Cybersecurity research
- Workflow automation
- Critical evaluation of AI outputs

## Key Takeaway

Generative AI can help evaluate business opportunities
and organize strategic decisions.

However, AI-generated estimates, recommendations,
and research findings require independent verification
before being used to make business decisions.
