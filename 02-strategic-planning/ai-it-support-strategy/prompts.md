# Project 03: AI-Powered IT Support Strategic Planning
## Gemini Prompt Documentation

### Prompt 1: Brainstorming AI Solutions

You are an AI solutions strategist specializing in IT support
and cybersecurity.

My company is developing an AI-powered IT support service
for small businesses.

Help me brainstorm 8 affordable AI-powered features or
services that could be offered to small businesses.

Focus on solutions that:
- Solve common IT support problems
- Reduce repetitive help desk tasks
- Improve cybersecurity awareness
- Are affordable for small businesses
- Can realistically be developed by someone entering
  the AI and IT support field

For each idea, briefly explain the problem it solves
and how AI would be used.

### Prompt 2: Cost-Benefit Analysis

Perform a cost-benefit analysis of the eight AI-powered
IT support solutions you suggested.

Format your response as a table with these columns:

1. AI Solution
2. Development Cost (Low/Medium/High)
3. Technical Difficulty (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
4. Business Benefit (Low/Medium/High)
5. Implementation Time
6. Portfolio Value for an Entry-Level AI/IT Professional

Assume I am an aspiring AI-enabled IT support professional
with limited programming experience and a small budget.

Identify the three solutions offering the highest benefit
at the lowest cost.

Explain which solution would be most realistic to build
as my first functional AI portfolio project.

Clearly distinguish estimated costs and timelines
from verified information.

### Prompt 3: Industry Research

Research current trends in AI-powered cybersecurity
solutions for small businesses, focusing on phishing
detection and email security.

Identify:

1. Five important industry trends in 2026.
2. How small businesses are adopting AI for email security.
3. Common limitations of AI phishing detection.
4. Opportunities for affordable AI-powered solutions.
5. Skills employers value in professionals developing
   or supporting these solutions.

For each trend, provide a credible source and
publication date.

Distinguish verified industry developments from
predictions or estimates.

Finally, explain how these trends could influence
the development of my proposed AI Phishing Analyzer.

### Prompt 4: Automated Industry Monitoring

Every Monday at 9:00 AM, research the latest developments
in AI-powered phishing detection and email security.

Focus on:
- Emerging phishing techniques
- AI-based email security tools
- Cybersecurity threats affecting small businesses
- New developments in phishing detection technology
- Skills relevant to AI-enabled IT support professionals

Summarize the five most important developments.

For each development, include:
1. A brief explanation
2. Why it matters to small businesses
3. How it could influence my AI Phishing Analyzer project
4. A credible source link and publication date

Clearly distinguish verified findings from predictions.
Do not repeat previously reported developments unless
there is significant new information.

---

## Prompt Engineering Techniques Demonstrated

- **Role prompting:** Assigned Gemini a professional role.
- **Contextual prompting:** Defined the target industry and audience.
- **Structured output:** Requested a comparison table.
- **Constraint setting:** Specified budget and experience limitations.
- **Research prompting:** Requested sources and publication dates.
- **Workflow automation:** Scheduled recurring research.

## Key Learning

I learned how to use AI to move from brainstorming
business ideas to evaluating options, researching
industry developments, and automating ongoing analysis.

I also learned the importance of verifying AI-generated
research and distinguishing estimates from established facts.
