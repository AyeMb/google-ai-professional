# Project 03: Strategic Planning — Results & Evaluation

## Project Overview

I used Google Gemini to brainstorm, compare, and evaluate
AI-powered IT support solutions for small businesses.

The objective was to identify a practical, affordable
solution that could potentially be developed into a
functional AI portfolio project.

---

## 3. Selected Solution: AI Phishing Analyzer

I selected the AI-Assisted Phishing Analyzer as a
potential future development project.

### Problem

Small business employees may struggle to identify
phishing emails and suspicious messages.

### Proposed Solution

An AI-powered assistant that:

- Reviews suspicious email content.
- Identifies potential phishing indicators.
- Explains warning signs in plain language.
- Recommends appropriate verification steps.
- Escalates uncertain or high-risk messages
  for human review.

### Why This Solution?

- Relevant to IT support and cybersecurity.
- Potentially affordable to prototype.
- Demonstrates practical AI applications.
- Builds on my earlier cybersecurity awareness project.
- Can be tested using simulated phishing emails.

### Important Limitation

AI analysis cannot guarantee that an email is safe.

The proposed system should assist human decision-making,
not replace established email security controls.

---

## 4. Industry Research Findings

Gemini identified five emerging email security trends:

### QR Code Phishing

Attackers may use QR codes to direct users to
malicious websites.

### Phishing-as-a-Service

Ready-made phishing tools can make attacks easier
to launch and distribute.

### Hybrid Voice and Email Attacks

Some social engineering campaigns combine email
messages with telephone-based deception.

### CAPTCHA-Based Evasion

Attackers may place malicious content behind
verification pages to complicate automated detection.

### API-Based Security Integration

API-based tools can integrate with cloud email
platforms and existing security workflows.

### Research Limitations

Gemini supplied specific statistics and publication
dates for these trends, but direct supporting sources
were not provided in the lab output.

The trends are recorded here as AI-generated research
findings requiring independent verification.

No unverified statistics are presented as established facts.
