## Project Overview

This project investigates emerging phishing techniques and their implications for small businesses.

Using Google Gemini Deep Research, I developed a research plan examining QR-code phishing (quishing), CAPTCHA-based evasion, and the limitations of traditional email security tools.

I then used AI to generate a comprehensive research report and transform its findings into an interactive cybersecurity infographic.

The research supports my ongoing exploration of a proposed AI-Assisted Phishing Analyzer.

## Research Objectives

- Investigate emerging visual and gated phishing techniques.
- Examine documented phishing trends from January 2024 through October 2026.
- Evaluate limitations of traditional and AI-powered detection methods.
- Explore affordable cybersecurity solutions for small businesses.
- Identify potential requirements for a future AI-assisted phishing detection application.
- Evaluate the reliability and limitations of AI-generated research.

## Methodology

## Key Research Findings

### 1. QR-Code Phishing (Quishing)

QR-code phishing can conceal malicious destinations within images, making certain attacks more difficult for text-focused security tools to identify.

The research examined how these attacks may redirect employees from corporate devices to personal smartphones.

### 2. CAPTCHA-Based Evasion

Attackers increasingly use fake verification pages and CAPTCHA-style challenges to manipulate users or conceal malicious activity.

The report also examined ClickFix attacks, which may trick users into executing malicious commands.

### 3. Traditional Email Security Limitations

Traditional security tools may struggle to identify threats hidden within images, attachments, or other forms of content obfuscation.

The research explored how additional inspection methods could strengthen phishing detection.

### 4. AI-Assisted Detection

The report proposed combining several technologies:

- QR-code extraction
- Computer vision
- Threat-intelligence APIs
- AI-assisted content analysis
- Human oversight

These capabilities represent a proposed detection architecture, not an implemented application.

### 5. Small-Business Cybersecurity

Small businesses may face challenges involving cybersecurity costs, limited technical resources, and employee awareness.

The research explored whether lightweight AI-assisted detection could provide an affordable supplementary defense.

## Critical Evaluation and Research Limitations

Although Gemini produced a detailed report and interactive infographic, I identified several limitations requiring further investigation.

### Source Verification

Some statistics were presented without sufficiently traceable citations.

Important figures should be independently verified against original government, academic, or reputable cybersecurity industry sources before being used in professional decision-making.

### AI Model Pricing

API pricing estimates may change over time.

The infographic's pricing figures require confirmation against official provider documentation.

### Cost Estimation

The interactive cost estimator uses assumptions about:

- Monthly email volume
- Token consumption
- AI model pricing
- Prompt caching
- Infrastructure requirements

Its outputs are illustrative estimates rather than validated operating costs.

### Technical Feasibility

The proposed detection architecture has not been implemented or tested.

Additional development would be necessary to evaluate detection accuracy, false positives, security, privacy, scalability, and operational costs.

### Responsible AI

This project reinforced the importance of critically evaluating AI-generated research rather than assuming that detailed or confidently presented information is accurate.

**The infographic is an AI-generated research visualization, not a verified cybersecurity assessment or functioning phishing detection system.**
