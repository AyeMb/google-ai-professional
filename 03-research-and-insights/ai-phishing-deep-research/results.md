# Project 06: Deep Research Results

## Research Topic

Visual and gated phishing techniques and their implications for small-business cybersecurity.

**Tool:** Google Gemini Deep Research  
**Research period:** January 2024–October 2026

## Research Findings

Gemini's research report examined several cybersecurity challenges:

### 1. QR-Code Phishing (Quishing)

Attackers can embed malicious links within QR codes to conceal destinations from text-focused security scanning.

### 2. CAPTCHA-Based Evasion

Attackers may use fake verification pages and CAPTCHA-style challenges to conceal malicious activity or manipulate users into unsafe actions.

### 3. Limitations of Traditional Email Security

Text-based scanning alone may fail to identify threats concealed within images, attachments, or compromised accounts.

### 4. AI-Assisted Detection

The report proposed combining QR-code extraction, threat-intelligence checks, and AI-assisted analysis to identify suspicious content.

These approaches remain proposed capabilities rather than tested features of my application.

### 5. Small-Business Considerations

The research highlighted the importance of affordability, manageable implementation requirements, employee awareness, and human oversight.

## Critical Evaluation

Although Gemini produced a detailed report and interactive visualization, several limitations require attention:

- Some statistics were presented without sufficiently traceable citations.
- API pricing figures require verification against official provider documentation.
- Cost estimates depend on assumptions about token usage, caching, and infrastructure.
- Technical recommendations require validation before implementation.
- AI-generated findings should not automatically be treated as verified evidence.

The infographic is retained as an AI-generated research artifact, not a validated cybersecurity assessment.
